# Bubble Pop

A webcam game for a TV: bubbles drift over your mirrored camera picture and up to four
players race to pop the most in 30 seconds.

**Play: https://cbaechle.github.io/bubble-game/** (desktop Chrome, a webcam, and room to jump)

Everything runs in your browser. The camera picture never leaves your computer.

This repository holds only the compiled site (build 4167e2d).
